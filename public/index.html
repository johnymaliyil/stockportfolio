How are stocks marked?
Each stock gets points from its last year of prices: +2/−2 above/below its 200-day average, +1/−1 above/below its 50-day average, +1/−1 50-day above/below the 200-day, +1/−1 3-month return above +5% / below −10%, +1/−1 beat/lagged the NIFTY 50 by 5%+ over the year, +1/−1 within 10% of / more than 40% below its 52-week high, and −1 each for penny stocks (under ₹10) and low liquidity (under ₹1 Cr traded a day). +3 or more = Strong, −3 or less = Weak, otherwise Neutral.
